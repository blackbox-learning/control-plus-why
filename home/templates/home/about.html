About Ctrl+Why

Where curiosity wins over practicality.

What is this?

Ctrl+Why is a playground for absurd software, unnecessary AI, overengineered experiments, and digital inventions nobody asked for. It's a place where the question "but why though?" is answered with "because we could."

Our philosophy

We build useless products with serious engineering. Some are funny. Some accidentally become useful. Most probably shouldn't exist. That's fine.

From emotionally unstable chatbots to APIs solving problems that were never problems in the first place, Ctrl+Why is where curiosity wins over practicality.

Why bother?

Because the best way to learn something is to build something unnecessary with it. Because constraints breed creativity, but so does the absence of them. Because sometimes the most interesting software is the software that has no business existing.

Who's behind this?

A person with too many ideas and not enough excuses to not build them. Ctrl+Why is an ongoing experiment — in software, in creativity, and in asking questions that probably don't have good answers.
